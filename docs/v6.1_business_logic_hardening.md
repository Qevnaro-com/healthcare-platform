## 1. Code Audit & Business Logic Hardening

- **B2C Intake Progressive Disclosure**:
  - Maintained the exact **4-step intake architecture** (`CARE → PATIENT & LOCATION → SCHEDULE & NEEDS → REVIEW & REQUEST`).
  - Hardened progressive disclosure so contextual questions appear **only** for the selected service category (e.g. surgical dressing & catheter tasks for Nursing Care; joint focus & mobility for Physiotherapy; complaint for Doctor Visit).
  - Sanitized patient inputs to prevent forcing both Age and Date of Birth simultaneously when not required.
  - Removed all internal operations data (Staff IDs, Employee IDs, Assignment IDs, Admin notes, internal cost/margins) from client-facing forms.
- **B2B Staffing Demand Model Hardening**:
  - Maintained the **4-section institutional intake architecture** (`01 REQUEST → 02 REQUIREMENTS → 03 LOCATION & SCHEDULE → 04 REVIEW & SUBMIT`).
  - **Removed organization-entered Daily Rate input fields** from the B2B intake form. Organizations request staffing capacity (e.g. Line 01: ICU Nurse × 4 + Line 02: Attendant × 2 = 6 requested roster slots). Pricing is derived from standard commercial terms rather than arbitrary user-entered rates.
  - Internal cost/margins remain strictly Admin-only.
- **Schedule & Recurrence Conditionality**:
  - If `scheduleType === 'single'`, weekly day selectors and recurrence configuration panels are automatically hidden.
  - If `scheduleType === 'recurring'`, weekday selectors (`Mon`, `Wed`, `Fri`) and total shift counters are dynamically revealed.
  - Night shifts calculate overnight date rollover automatically (e.g. 10:00 PM to 08:00 AM next day).

## 4. Pricing Reality Classification

- **Determination**: Real deterministic pricing calculation logic in React state & `BookingContext`.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Base Rate} = \text{Base Price} \times \text{Shift Multiplier} \times \text{Days Count}$$
  $$\text{Night Surcharge} = \text{Subtotal} \times 0.20 \quad (\text{if Night Shift})$$
  $$\text{GST} = \text{Subtotal} \times 0.18$$
  $$\text{Grand Total} = \text{Subtotal} + \text{Night Surcharge} + \text{GST}$$
- **Transparency**: Displayed itemized in Step 04 (Review & Request). No hidden cash demands.

## 6. Role Permission & Data Isolation Audit

- **Client Isolation**: Clients see only their own bookings, patient profiles, and invoices. Zero access to other clients or B2B orgs.
- **Organization Isolation**: Organizations see only their own facility requests, roster slots, schedule, timesheets, and invoices (**Org A ≠ Org B isolation enforced**). Zero marketplace candidate browsing or bidding.
- **Admin Operations**: Full cross-platform visibility over client care requests and institutional staffing requests.
- **Staff Duty Isolation**: Internal employees see only assigned duties, shift schedules, timesheets, and duty-necessary patient context.
